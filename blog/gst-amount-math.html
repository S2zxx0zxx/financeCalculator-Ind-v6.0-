How inclusive and exclusive GST arithmetic works

Separate the arithmetic from the rate decision: you must verify which GST rate and classification apply.

GST arithmeticFinCalc EditorialPublished 27 Sep 2026

When the starting amount is before GST

If the pre-tax value is B and the rate you have verified is t percent, GST is B × t ÷ 100. Add that amount to the base to get the total.

With a ₹100 base and an entered 18% rate, the arithmetic gives ₹18 tax and a ₹118 total.

When the amount already includes GST

Divide the gross amount by 1 + t ÷ 100 to recover the base. The GST component is gross amount minus that base.

₹118 including an entered 18% rate gives a ₹100 base and ₹18 GST.

Try either direction in the GST amount splitter.

Choosing the correct rate is separate

This calculator does not classify a product or service, determine exemptions, place of supply, or decide whether CGST/SGST or IGST applies. Confirm classification and applicable rate using the official CBIC GST rate schedule and, where needed, a qualified tax professional.

Scope: Formula explanation only. This is not a live rate, forecast, product offer, filing guide or personalized recommendation.

Example assumptions
