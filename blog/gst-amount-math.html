How inclusive and exclusive GST arithmetic works

Separate a user-entered GST percentage from product classification and explain both calculation directions.

The arithmetic for GST depends on whether the amount you start with is before tax or already includes tax. This calculator does not determine which rate applies to a product or service. Confirm classification and rate with the official CBIC rate schedule.

Amount before GST

If the pre-tax amount is B and the percentage is t, tax is B × t ÷ 100; the total is B × (1 + t ÷ 100). At ₹100 and an entered 18%, tax is ₹18 and the total is ₹118.

Amount including GST

If the total already includes GST, divide it by 1 + t ÷ 100 to recover the pre-tax amount. For ₹118 at an entered 18%, the base is ₹100 and the included tax is ₹18.

Try both modes in the GST amount splitter. It does not determine CGST/SGST versus IGST, exemptions, place of supply, invoicing or filing obligations.
