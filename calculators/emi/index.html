How this estimate works

EMI = principal × monthly rate ÷ (1 − (1 + monthly rate)−number of months). At 0% interest, the principal is divided equally by the number of months. The result assumes a fixed nominal annual rate divided by 12, equal payments at the end of each month, and no changes to the rate or term.

Estimate only. Processing fees, insurance, taxes, prepayments, variable rates and lender rounding are excluded. Confirm terms and the repayment schedule with your lender before committing.
