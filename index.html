What FinCalc does and does not know

Calculators use your inputs and displayed formulas. We do not fetch live lender or bank offers. The income-tax illustration covers only a narrow verified salary case. We cannot read or calculate your actual CIBIL score; check the bureau directly.

Saved scenarios stay in this browser and can be exported. Your browser can remove local data, so keep a backup if a scenario matters.
