Privacy Policy

How FinCalc India collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

📋 Introduction

Welcome to FinCalc India ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible at satzzxzxx.me. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using FinCalc India, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

📊 Calculator data and local storage

New FinCalc calculators run in your browser without an account. Your calculator inputs are not sent to a FinCalc server by those tools. If you choose Save, the scenario name, assumptions and result are stored in this browser's IndexedDB. You can view, delete or export them from Saved. A JSON export is stored wherever you choose on your device. Clearing site data may erase local scenarios.

Theme preference is stored in browser localStorage. Service-worker caches keep pages and code available offline. Older site versions may have left local keys for calculator history, newsletter form state and draft contact messages; those keys are not evidence that a message was delivered or a subscription created. We do not upload them automatically.

Browser requests to our hosting provider can produce ordinary access and security logs outside the calculator's control. Avoid entering passwords, bank credentials, account numbers or OTPs into calculators or exported scenario names.

🍪 Analytics and ads

The redesigned calculator pages do not load Google Analytics, ad networks or marketing pixels. Older blog and site pages have had their external analytics and advertising loaders paused while a proper consent and disclosure flow is reviewed. We do not claim a newsletter subscription until a real mail service exists.

🔗 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (such as bank websites, government portals, or financial news sites). We have no control over the content and privacy practices of those websites, and they are not covered by this Privacy Policy. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit.

🔒 Data Security

All financial calculations on FinCalc India are performed entirely in your browser (client-side JavaScript). A saved scenario stays locally in your browser until you delete or export it; our current calculators do not upload its financial inputs.

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we do collect, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

👶 Children's Privacy

FinCalc India is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

🌍 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued use of FinCalc India after any changes constitutes acceptance of the new Privacy Policy.
